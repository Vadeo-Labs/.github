# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for a security problem.

Report it privately by either route:

- Use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** button on the repository's _Security_ tab, when it is enabled.
- Or write to [hola@vadeo-labs.com](mailto:hola@vadeo-labs.com) with the subject `Security report`.

Include what you found, where, the steps to reproduce it and the impact you see. Never include real customer data.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within **three business days**.
- We keep you informed while we confirm and fix it, and tell you when it is resolved.
- We ask that you give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it.

## Scope

Everything the studio ships: its website, its products (Asistto, Vereda Viva), client sites we build and the
`@vadeo-labs/*` packages. Findings in third-party services are best reported to those vendors.
