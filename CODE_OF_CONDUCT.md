# Code of conduct

Vadeo Labs works in the open with clients, teammates and the people who use our software. These rules keep that
space useful and respectful for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and direct. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- Assume good intent, ask when something is unclear, and give feedback that helps the work move.
- Welcome people of every background, language (Spanish and English), experience level and ability.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and credit.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats, in any form.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Spam, or anything that disrupts the work of others.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other space where people act for or with Vadeo Labs.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to [hola@vadeo-labs.com](mailto:hola@vadeo-labs.com). We review every report promptly and keep it
confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may restrict participation, when conduct breaks these
rules.
