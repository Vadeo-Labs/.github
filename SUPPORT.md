# Support

## Questions about our products or packages

- Check the repository's `README.md` and `docs/` first.
- For a bug or a request, open an issue with the matching template.
- For anything else, write to [hola@vadeo-labs.com](mailto:hola@vadeo-labs.com). We reply within one business day.

## Security problems

Do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Working with us

Tell us about your project at [vadeo-labs.com](https://www.vadeo-labs.com) or by email. After a 45-minute call you get a
proposal with scope, timeline and a fixed price.
