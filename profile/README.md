## Why "Vadeo"?

_Vado_ is Spanish for **ford**: the shallow place where a river can be crossed. That is how we work. We find the
simplest way across, from the problem to working software, and we skip the detours.

## We eat our own cooking

Two products in daily use, built and maintained by us. They are the proof of how we work.

|                                       | What it does                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Asistto**<br/>_WhatsApp booking_    | An AI assistant for barbershops, spas, clinics and workshops. It answers clients on WhatsApp, books, confirms and reminds appointments while the owner works. |
| **Vereda Viva**<br/>_Citizen reports_ | One WhatsApp number to report problems in your municipality. The AI organizes the report, issues a tracking number and sends it to the responsible office.    |

## Client work

Sites and tools we design and build for other companies, from the idea to the deploy.

- **VARE**: identity, logo and a bilingual site for a Colombian company that invests in oil palm and real estate.
- **Vargas & Arenas**: accounting-firm site in Bogotá with a tax calendar, free calculators and a contact form.
- **Postall Overlander**: bilingual site of an overlanding stop in Guatapé, with directions and requests on WhatsApp.

## How a project goes

```text
Week 1        Understand   45-minute call, process map and a proposal with scope, timeline and fixed price
Weeks 2 to 5  Build        A delivery every Friday and a 30-minute live review with you
Week 6        Launch       Launch, measurement, code and documentation included
```

A first working product usually takes four to eight weeks. You watch it grow and decide at every step.

## What you can count on

- **Fixed price.** After the first call you get a proposal, not an open meter.
- **Deliveries every Friday.** No surprises, no month of silence.
- **You own the code.** At the end we hand over the repository, the documentation and access to everything we built.
- **Built for everyone.** WCAG 2.2 AA, Spanish and English, phone first.
- **Small teams, real results.** We work with teams that need an outcome, not meetings.

## How we build

React · TypeScript · Node.js · MongoDB and job queues · OpenAI models · Vercel and other clouds.

Our shared engineering foundations (lint, format and TypeScript presets, server and web cores) live in this
organization as `@vadeo-labs/*` packages and ship with every project, so a client gets the same discipline the products get.
